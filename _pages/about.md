Hi, I'm Puranjay! I'm an incoming MS student in Computer Science at **Texas A&M University**. I earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science, *cum laude*, from **Drexel University** in 2026.

I'm drawn to **machine learning**, especially where it meets other fields. I'm most excited by interdisciplinary work, where computational methods help solve real problems in another domain. I'm still discovering exactly where that intersection lies for me, and I'm looking forward to exploring it during my graduate studies.

**Experience**

Across co-ops and internships, I've built data and AI pipelines in a range of domains. Most recently, at **XelerAIT**, I built an automated pipeline that turns the Indian Council of Medical Research's unstructured treatment guidelines into a clinical ontology, using vision-based LLM extraction. Before that, I used prompt-engineered LLM pipelines at **GSK** to standardize definitions for roughly 5,000 data attributes. At **SEPTA**, I built tools and dashboards that streamlined Philadelphia's transit ridership reporting. At **Aon**, I automated the processing of global exposure data, cutting preprocessing time from an hour to seconds.

I've also worked on NLP methods for structuring complex legal text in a **Bloomberg**-sponsored senior design project, and built **PlasmaCast**, an XGBoost model that forecasts plasma donor demand across 10 U.S. cities.

**Outside of work**

When I'm away from the screen, you'll usually find me behind a camera. Photography is one of my favorite ways to unwind. I also enjoy playing basketball and pickleball whenever I get the chance, reading about how the human body works, and cooking, where I'm always trying out new recipes.
